# Character Briefs

## Nasreddin Hoca

Preferred method: approved multi-view concept image to Image-to-3D.

Prompt:

> respectful stylized-realistic Nasreddin Hoca guide character for a child-friendly Turkish cultural exploration game, warm intelligent expression, traditional modest layered clothing, white turban, short tidy white beard, clear hands and separate limbs, standard humanoid proportions, neutral A-pose, feet flat and symmetric, suitable for automatic rigging, clean topology, consistent with the global style anchor; no donkey attached, no text, no caricature, no oversized head

Required animations after rigging:

- idle
- walk
- wave
- point
- talk gesture
- collect
- celebrate
