# Art Bible

## Locked visual direction

**Stylized realistic 3D for children.**

The world should have believable materials, scale, lighting and recognizable Turkish architecture, but simplified shapes and slightly softened proportions. Avoid both toy-like plastic minimalism and photorealistic digital-twin ambition.

## Shape language

- clear silhouettes;
- rounded or lightly softened edges where historically appropriate;
- large readable primary forms;
- limited micro-detail;
- props should remain identifiable on a small mobile screen;
- no floating labels baked into models.

## Material language

- PBR materials;
- restrained roughness variation;
- stone, wood, painted metal, copper, ceramic, fabric and vegetation must read distinctly;
- avoid uniform glossy surfaces;
- use decals or trim sheets for repeated detail where practical.

## Color

The legacy palette remains a UI and regional identity reference:

- çini blue `#1B7FA8`
- turquoise `#3EC6C9`
- night blue `#16324F`
- flag red `#E0322F`
- gold `#F2B233`
- warm cream `#FFF8E7`
- green `#4CAF7D`

The 3D environment may use natural material colors. Regional colors should appear through accents, UI and lighting rather than tinting entire cities.

## Lighting

- one clear time-of-day per city;
- soft sun and ambient fill;
- baked or lightweight shadows where possible;
- avoid heavy cinematic bloom;
- no dark horror-like interiors;
- underground scenes require safe, warm guiding light.

## Characters

Nasreddin Hoca and Keloğlan must:

- share the same art style and scale;
- use standard humanoid topology suitable for rigging;
- have expressive but not exaggerated faces;
- avoid text or logos;
- be respectful cultural interpretations, not caricatures;
- include clean hands and separate readable limbs;
- be created in neutral pose for rigging, then animated.

## Architecture accuracy

- Historical monuments use approved visual references.
- Image-to-3D is preferred for recognizable monuments.
- Do not invent Arabic/Ottoman inscriptions.
- Do not add unrelated crosses, coats of arms, flags or religious ornament.
- Remove or simplify unreadable generated text.
- Hero assets require human visual review before use.

## Asset tiers

### Hero

Landmark or guide character viewed closely.

### Midground

Shopfront, stall, large prop or secondary structure.

### Background

Silhouette, modular facade, distant terrain or crowd card.

### Collectible

Small close-up object with clean turntable presentation.

## Environment construction

Do not ask Meshy to create an entire city street as one model. Build scenes from:

- shared props;
- one regional kit;
- a small number of city identity assets;
- a hero landmark where justified;
- distant low-cost skyline or terrain.
