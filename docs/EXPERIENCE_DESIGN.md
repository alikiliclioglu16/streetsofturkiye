# Experience Design

## Experience tone

A warm museum adventure rather than a simulation game. The child should feel free to move and discover, while the route remains curated enough to avoid getting lost.

## Standard city rhythm

### Arrival — 10–15 seconds

- wide establishing camera;
- city and region title;
- one guide greeting;
- visual reveal of the short route;
- skip action always available.

### Discovery — approximately 3 minutes

Three strong hotspots. Each hotspot must use the sequence:

1. visual callout;
2. approach and camera framing;
3. interaction;
4. concise fact;
5. collectible;
6. visible route progress.

### Quiz gate — 20–40 seconds

- one or two questions;
- questions recall observed or performed details;
- wrong answers provide retry without punishment;
- completion awards the province star.

### Exit — 10 seconds

- collection recap;
- province star;
- region progress;
- return to map or continue action.

## Camera

- Default third-person or close-follow camera.
- Use authored camera anchors at hotspots.
- Avoid abrupt cuts during user control.
- Clamp pitch to child-friendly viewing angles.
- Avoid motion sickness: no head bob, no forced rapid rotation.
- Guided mode may use more cinematic movement but must respect reduced-motion settings.

## UI hierarchy

Persistent in-city UI should be minimal:

- city name and route progress;
- backpack/collection count;
- settings/pause;
- contextual interaction prompt.

Long information sheets must not cover the entire scene unless paused. Use short, readable panels with optional “learn more.”

## Feedback

- Success uses sound, small animation and object response.
- Failure is gentle and immediately retryable.
- Reward objects replace legacy emoji-only rewards.
- Every actionable object must have a clear hover/focus/touch affordance.

## Pilot city direction

### İstanbul

Mood: bright Bosphorus morning, blue water, seagulls, ferry horn, urban stone and İznik blue accents.

Learning verbs: observe a tile, inspect a tower, recognize city geography.

### Nevşehir

Mood: warm sunrise, soft volcanic stone, balloons in slow motion, quiet wind.

Learning verbs: inspect erosion forms, assemble a pottery motif, observe underground design.

### Gaziantep

Mood: warm yellow-stone bazaar, copper rhythm, pistachio green details, intimate lanes.

Learning verbs: identify mosaic detail, select baklava ingredients, repeat a coppersmith rhythm.
